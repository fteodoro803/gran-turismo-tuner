# DECISIONS.md

The log of decisions made about this project, and why. Open questions live on GitHub as issues labelled `decision`, where anyone can weigh in and the owner makes the call. Once a call is made, it's recorded here.

## What belongs here

- **This file**: decisions that were made, with the reasoning, so later work doesn't reopen them by accident.
- **Not here**:
  - An open question → a `decision` issue.
  - Work a decision creates → its own issue (`known-issue`, `deferred` or `bug`).
  - A convention a decision creates → `CLAUDE.md`, linked from the row.

| D8 | **Car data comes from the owner's own screenshots of the official list, written to a flat `cars.json` with ~~our own readable slug ids~~ our own whole-number ids (D15) and no Sony id field.** An id is never changed once it has appeared in a shared link. No scraping. | `CLAUDE.md` |
| D9 | **Tuning form (from #6): number inputs take free typing and clamp and round on blur or Enter, an empty field means unset, a temporary "Drivetrain (preview)" dropdown feeds the rules until the car picker (#12) lands, and component tests use Testing Library.** The torque split and gear list wait for #13. Styling and layout are expected to change in the design pass (#20), so they are not recorded as decided. | `src/components/NumberInput.tsx` |
| D10 (follow-up to D1) | **Two reference screenshots of the game's tuning screens are committed under `docs/reference/`, for checking the layout and grouping only.** D1 still holds for the app: no Sony or manufacturer images are used in the code, the build or `dist/`. The app hides a picture that has no path or whose file is missing, so a build without the folder is text only. | D12 (from #12) | **The first page and the tuning page are switched by in-memory state, not by the URL.** The share-link format (#5) puts the tune after the `#`, so a page in the URL would clash with it. A refresh returns to the first page, and the tune is kept when you change the car. | `src/App.tsx` |
| D13 (from #20) | **The tuning form is one page with no tabs: every section, in schema order, flows down CSS columns, and the page picks the column count and the size at runtime so a tablet or larger (768px wide and up) never scrolls.** Phones scroll normally, there is no minimum text size, Tires sits directly above Suspension as one block, there is no top bar (the car and drivetrain are in General, with a Back button at the top left), and Engine Tuning and Bodywork stay one toggle per row, like the game. This replaces the game's two-sheet layout. Text and touch boxes get small on small screens, which the owner accepted (tracked in #24). | `src/components/useFitToScreen.ts`, `src/components/fit.ts`, `src/components/TuneForm.tsx` |
| D14 (from #20) | **The look is dark only: the Slate palette (a mid blue-grey), a Sky accent, panels with a cut top-left corner and a tick before the heading, and Rajdhani at weight 700 for all text.** Lighter weights read too thin. The font is self-hosted through `@fontsource/rajdhani`, so the app makes no request to Google. Nothing is copied from the game: the colours, corner and font are our own (D1). | `src/index.css`, `src/main.tsx` |
| D15 (follow-up to D8) | **A car's id is a whole number, 1 to 580 in the order of the car list, and not a slug of its name.** Every shared link carries the id, and a slug of up to about 60 characters made links long where D2 wants them compact. A number is also safe from renames. The cost is that `cars.json` no longer says which car an id is without reading `name`. Done before the link codec (#5) wrote any link, so no old link needed migrating. The picture files keep their old slug names, and each car's `image` field stays the only link between a car and its picture (D11). A new car takes the next number up, and gaps are never filled. | `CLAUDE.md`, `src/data/cars.json` |
| D16 (follow-up to D12) | **A tune always has a car: the car picker has no "My car isn't listed" option, and `CarChoice` is `{ car }`.** The drivetrain is always `car.drivetrain`, so the link needs no drivetrain of its own and a link for a car that isn't in the list is simply unreadable. | `src/components/CarPicker.tsx` |
| D17 (follow-up to D2, from #5) | **A share link is plain text: `version,car id,toggles,field:value,…`.** Each field is named by a permanent number `n` in `schema.ts`. All toggles that are on share one base 36 number (bit `n - 1`). An enum is the position of its option, a number is itself, and gears are `g1/g2/g3~final`. Defaults are left out. A link that cannot be read (not a link, an unknown version, or a car id not in the list) decodes to `undefined`, while a bad value in one field is dropped and the rest opens. There is no unit slot yet: units arrive with #14 and bump the version to 2. Chosen over JSON in base64, which made a worst-case link 2,735 characters (about 894 with numeric keys) against 458 now, and a typical one about 100 to 200. The cost is that field numbers and enum option order become part of the format. | `src/tune/codec.ts`, `src/tune/schema.ts`, `CLAUDE.md` |
| D18 (supersedes D5, from #15) | **A shared link opens straight into the normal editable form, and anyone can change it.** There is no read-only page and no "Edit a copy" button. The tuning page has a Copy link button, and the address bar is not updated as you edit. | `src/App.tsx`, `src/components/CopyLinkButton.tsx` |
| D19 (follow-up to D1 and D11) | **While no car is chosen, the picker's picture area shows a big grey question mark, not a car silhouette.** A silhouette made from one of Sony's pictures is still Sony artwork, so it can't ship (D1) and would be missing wherever the git-ignored pictures are (D11). A drawn outline was tried and dropped as less car-like. The area has a fixed height, so Enter never moves. | `src/components/CarPlaceholder.tsx` |
| D20 (follow-up to D19) | **The picker's picture area shows nothing, not even the question mark, until there are pictures of our own.** The release is text only (D1, D11), so the live site has no pictures, and a big question mark in an empty card looked like something was broken. The card is still drawn at its fixed height, so Enter never moves and a picture can drop in later. The question mark and its style are removed. A part of D19 that stays: the fixed height. Along with this, the fixed-height rules that a merge had nested inside `.button` in `src/index.css` were moved back out, so the height applies again. | `src/components/CarPlaceholder.tsx`, `src/index.css` |
| D21 (follow-up to D3 and D17, from #14) | **Units are three rows of buttons at the bottom of General, one button per unit. A number is stored in the unit it was typed in and only the display converts; typing a number makes the shown unit its stored unit.** Share link version 2 adds `u:<power><torque><weight>` (the position of each unit, left out when all are the defaults), and version 1 links still open with the defaults. The shown unit is the viewer's choice saved in `localStorage`, falling back to the author's. Conversion goes through one base unit per family and rounds to the nearest step of the field's decimals, which is provisional until the research in #9 and #11 says what the game does. Unit option order is permanent, like enum options. | `src/tune/units.ts`, `src/tune/codec.ts`, `CLAUDE.md` |
| D22 (follow-up to D21, from #14) | **Design pass on the form controls: labels use Rajdhani 500 in the muted colour and values and buttons use 600 (the 700 weight is no longer loaded); unit choices are plain text with the chosen one in the accent colour and underlined; dropdowns stay as they were; number boxes are 2px shorter with a softer placeholder grey; toggles are a hand-drawn square checkbox that fills with the accent and a dark tick.** Chosen from three options each on a design canvas. The font change applies to the whole app, including the car picker. | `src/index.css`, `src/main.tsx` |

## How to use this file

- **Record** a decision as the next `D#`: bold the one-line decision, put the reasoning after it, and note "(from #N)" if it came from a `decision` issue. Fill "Documented in" with the file(s) where the rule now lives.
- **Close the issue** it came from with a comment pointing here, and unblock anything that said "Blocked by" it.
- **Never renumber.** Gaps are fine; other docs cross-reference `D#`.
- **Follow-ups** to a decision get their own row, labelled `D# (follow-up)`, rather than an edit that changes what the original said.
- Companion docs: `PROJECT_CONTEXT.md`, `CLAUDE.md`, and the repo's issues.
